Before you put it in front of your sales team.

Where does the information come from?+

Trade mark records come from the UK Intellectual Property Office. Company details come from Companies House. Public web evidence helps assess maturity and retail presence. Source URLs are retained so your team can check the work.

Is this confirmed buying intent?+

No. LaunchTrace identifies an early commercial signal and infers what a brand at that stage typically needs. It does not claim the company is currently buying, has a budget or will purchase.

What does the score mean?+

The 0–100 LaunchTrace Score ranks how early-stage and commercially relevant a filing appears. Every score includes its reasons; it is a prioritisation tool, not a probability.

Is this a trade mark monitoring service?+

No. It is a sales-intelligence feed. LaunchTrace does not monitor infringement, provide legal advice or replace the official trade mark register.

Do you contact the brands for us?+

No. Your team decides whether and how to approach each opportunity. LaunchTrace gives you the signal and supporting evidence.

Can I cancel whenever I want?+

Yes. There is no annual contract or notice period. Cancellation stops future billing and you keep access for the period already paid.
