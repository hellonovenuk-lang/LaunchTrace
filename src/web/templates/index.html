How it works

Five stages, run once a week, with the whole funnel recorded so we can show our working.

1Trade mark activity — the week's accepted UK filings from the IPO journal
2Company verification — matched to Companies House, with a confidence score
3Launch research — website, retail listings and how established the brand already is
4Opportunity score — explainable 0–100 signal with the reasons behind it
5Friday feed — email plus CSV, ready for your sales team

FAQ

Where does the data come from?

Trade mark records come from the UK Intellectual Property Office's weekly Trade Marks Journal and the IPO's Open Data release. Company details come from Companies House. Both are published under the Open Government Licence v3.0. Website and retail evidence comes from public web search, and we keep the URLs so you can check anything yourself.

Is this a trade mark watching service?

No. LaunchTrace is a sales-intelligence feed. We don't monitor for infringement, we don't give legal advice, and we're not a trade mark database.

What does the LaunchTrace Score mean?

It's our own 0–100 signal of how early-stage and how commercially relevant a filing looks. It is not a probability that the company will buy anything. Every score comes with the reasons behind it so you can disagree with it.

Does "packaging relevance: HIGH" mean they're buying packaging?

No. It means a brand at this stage, in this category, typically needs that kind of supplier. It is an inference about likely need, not an observation of current purchasing.

How many opportunities per week?

It varies with what the IPO publishes. We ran the pipeline over historical journal weeks before launch precisely so we could answer this with evidence rather than a guess — ask us for the validation figures.

Can I cancel?

Any time, from the link in every email. No contract and no notice period.

Do you email the brands on my behalf?

No. LaunchTrace gives your team the signal. Any outreach is yours to make.
