## 3. The nature of the information

Everything we provide is derived from public sources and from our own judgement
about them.

- The **LaunchTrace Score** is our opinion of how early-stage and commercially
  relevant a filing appears. It is not a prediction that any company will make
  a purchase, and it carries no guarantee of accuracy.
- **Supplier relevance bands** (for example "packaging relevance: HIGH")
  indicate what a brand at that stage typically needs. They are **not**
  statements that the company is currently buying, has a budget, or is in
  market.
- Companies House matches carry a confidence score. A match is our best
  assessment, not a certainty.
- Public registers contain errors and lag reality. We do not warrant that any
  record is accurate, current or complete.

You are responsible for your own commercial decisions and for verifying
