# Email 1 — first contact

Send from your own mailbox, one at a time. Replace every bracket. Use **real**
opportunities from this week's run that genuinely suit what this company makes.

---

**Subject:** A few new food brands that may be useful

contract manufacturing / co-packing / fulfilment] you provide, so I thought I'd
send them over.

- **[BRAND NAME]** — [product category], [company name], incorporated
  [X months] ago. [One line on why they fit what this prospect supplies.]
- **[BRAND NAME]** — [product category], [company name], [region].
  [One line.]
- **[BRAND NAME]** — [product category], [company name], [region].
  [One line.]

Best,

---

## Before you send

- [ ] The three brands are real, from this week's run, and suit **this**
      prospect's product
- [ ] The company details match `opportunities.csv` exactly
- [ ] This address is not in `suppressions.csv`
- [ ] Your name, company and an opt-out line are all present

## What not to do

Do not claim these companies are buying anything. You are saying they exist and
are early. That is the whole and sufficient point.
