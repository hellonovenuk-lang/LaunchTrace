# Email 3 — one follow-up

Send once, about a week after the sample. If there is no reply, stop. Mark
`reply_status` as `none` and move on.

---

**Subject:** Worth keeping the feed going?

Best,
[YOUR NAME]

## After this

**Stop.** One first contact, one sample, one follow-up. If there is no reply
after this, they are not interested right now and a fourth email will only cost
you the relationship.

If they say no, record why in `notes`. That is the most valuable output of this
whole exercise: a pattern of "the brands are too early for us" means something
quite different from "we already know about these", and each points at a
different fix.

If they opt out, add them to `suppressions.csv` immediately and set `status` to
`suppressed`.
